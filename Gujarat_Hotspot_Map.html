Hotspots are ranked within their own city on listing volume, then split by average price per sqft into High, Medium and Low value. Areas of interest lie within 6 km of a High or Medium value hotspot — a Low value hotspot does not seed one. Blue dots show focus accounts, sized by how many trade there. Positions are approximate locality centres, not surveyed boundaries.
